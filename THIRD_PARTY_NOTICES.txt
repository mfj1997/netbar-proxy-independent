This distribution includes a Python runtime, PySide6/Qt, Shiboken6 and the
PyInstaller bootloader. They retain their upstream copyrights and licenses.
The application dynamically uses the bundled Qt libraries. Complete Python
application source and a rebuild script are supplied in source/, allowing
users to build with their chosen compatible Qt/PySide6 libraries.
